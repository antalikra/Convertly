Convert iPhone HEIC photos to JPG in seconds, right in your browser. Nothing is uploaded: Convertly converts files on your own computer, so your photos and documents stay private.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Click the Convertly icon in the toolbar. A converter window opens.
2. Drop your HEIC photos into it, or click to choose them.
3. Click Convert, then download the JPGs one by one or all at once as a ZIP.

WHY CONVERTLY
• Private: files never leave your computer. No uploads, no account, no tracking.
• Batches: up to 200 files at a time, several converted in parallel.
• Location removed: converted photos carry no GPS or other EXIF data.
• Sensible defaults: 90% quality, no visible difference and far smaller files than 100%.
• Free, no watermarks, no sign-up. Works offline.
• Available in 14 languages.

MORE THAN HEIC
• Images: HEIC, HEIF, JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP, AVIF, TIFF and SVG to JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, TIFF, BMP or a single PDF. Quality and resize controls.
• Audio: MP3, WAV, FLAC, M4A, AAC and OGG to MP3, WAV or OGG. Trim, mono and normalize.
• PDF: merge, split, rotate, compress, keep or reorder pages, add a watermark or page numbers, convert to JPG, PNG, text or DOCX.
• Word: DOCX to PDF, text or HTML (beta).

PERMISSIONS
Only "storage", to remember your settings. Convertly cannot read the websites you visit.

Open source: https://github.com/antalikra/Convertly
HEIC decoding uses libheif through heic-to, and MP3 encoding uses LAME through lamejs (both LGPL-3.0, shipped unmodified).
