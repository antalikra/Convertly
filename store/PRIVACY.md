# Convertly privacy policy

Effective date: 16 October 2026

Convertly is a browser extension that converts images, audio and documents. It works entirely on your device.

## Your files

When you drop or choose files, Convertly reads them in the extension's own window and converts them there, on your computer. Converted files are offered to you as downloads. Files and results are kept only in memory while the window is open and are gone when you clear the list or close the window. They are never uploaded, sent or shared: Convertly makes no network requests.

Converted images are re-encoded from their pixels, so they carry no EXIF metadata such as GPS location or camera details.

## What is stored

Convertly saves its settings in the browser's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`) on your device: the chosen output formats, quality, resize, audio and PDF options, the theme, how many batches have finished, and whether you have answered the one-time request to rate the extension. Nothing else is stored, and none of it leaves your device. Removing the extension deletes it.

## What is not collected

No analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no accounts and no cookies. Convertly does not read the websites you visit: its only permission is `storage`.

## Links

The support menu contains links to the Chrome Web Store review page, Ko-fi and PayPal. They open in a new tab only when you click them; those sites have their own privacy policies.

## Limited Use

Convertly's use of the data it handles adheres to the [Chrome Web Store User Data Policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/), including the Limited Use requirements. The data is used only to provide the conversion you asked for. It is never sold, transferred to third parties or used for any other purpose.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new effective date.
